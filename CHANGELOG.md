## 0.2.0 — 2026-10-16

- Practice is a third lesson page. Twelve programs cover `library` and `::`, a scatter, a histogram, a boxplot, a fitted line, and summaries: mean and median, standard deviation, `table`, correlation, and a paired test.
- Try it shows the plot when a program draws one, next to any printed result.
- The header version chip is v0.2.0.
- The public site is https://jmaret.github.io/Reticulum/. The Pages build prefixes assets, including the icon, with `/Reticulum`.
- Try it shows the program's stdout and stderr. Package messages and other conditions stay out of that text.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-16

- First room. Thirteen lessons run from the top of a script through copy-on-modify, environments, `NA`, `NULL`, and errors.
- A further page of eleven lessons: an S3 method, one generic for two classes, a nested function, `::`, `lapply`, a function that returns a function, a mixed list, a factor, a data frame, a formula with `lm`, and `NA` inside `mean`.
- Each lesson has a check graded in the browser, and Try it runs that program with WebR in this browser.
- A page on the language: where R came from, how it compares with Python, Julia, and SQL, why someone chooses it, and where it has a following.
- Vision and architecture pages show the summary of the living documents. The architecture page also shows the conceptual, logical, and physical pictures.
- The header version chip opens these notes. The version chip is v0.1.0.
- The backdrop is stars, math, and statistics.
